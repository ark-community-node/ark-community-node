# Contributing

Welcome, especially **native speakers of Swahili and other Kenyan/African languages**, who can
review benchmark items and grade model answers. That's the most valuable contribution right now.

- Code: open a PR with tests (`cd gateway && node --test`).
- Benchmark items: edit `bench/data/*.jsonl`. Every item needs a `reviewed_by` field before it counts.
- By contributing you agree your work is licensed under Apache-2.0.
- Be kind. Assume good faith.
